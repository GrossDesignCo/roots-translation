
hoy choshvei-aven uPoalei ra al-mishkevotam beOr haBoqer yaasuha ki yesh-leEl yadam

veChamdu sadot veGazalu uBatim veNasau veAshequ gever uBeito veIsh veNachalato

lakhen koh amar YHWH hineni choshev al-haMishpachah haZot raah asher lo-tamishu miSham tzavroteikhem veLo telekhu romah ki et raah hi

baYom haHu yissa aleykhem mashal veNahah nehi nihyah amar shadod neshadunu cheleq ammi yamir eikh yamish li leShovev sadeinu yechalleq

lakhen lo-yihyeh lekha maShlikh chevel beGoral biQahal YHWH

al-taTtifu yaTtifun lo-yaTtifu leEleh lo yiSsag kelimot

heAmur beit-Yaaqov haQatsar ruach YHWH im-eleh maAlalav halo devarai yeitivu im haYashar holekh

veEtmol ammi leOyev yeQomem miMul salmah eder taPshitun meOverim betach shuvei milchamah

neshei ammi teGarshun miBeit taanugeiha meAl olaleiha tiQchu hadari leOlam

qumu uLekhu ki lo-zot haMenuchah baavur tameah teChabel veChevel nimrats

lu-ish holekh ruach vaSheqer kizzev attif lekha laYayin veLaShekhar veHayah maTtif haAm haZeh

asof eEsof Yaaqov kullakh qabets eQabets sheerit Yisrael yachad asimennu keTzon Botsrah keEder beTokh haDovro teHimenah meAdam

alah haPoretz liFneihem partzu vaYaavru shaar vaYetzeu bo vaYaavor malkam liFneihem vaYHWH beRosham

"Devastating, we have been devastated; he will change
the portion of my people; how he will displace to me! to returning our fields he will allot."

in the congregation of He-Who-Is (YHWH)."

is the Wind of He-Who-Is (YHWH) short? 
if these, his deeds?
is it not my words do-well with the straight walking?

And yesterday my people to an enemy will stand-up, 
from front of a cloak, a mantle you will strip;
from those passing-through in security, returners of war.

The women of my people you will banish from the house of her luxuries;
from over her infants you will take my honor to the-age.

Stand-up and go, that this is not the resting-place;
for the sake of unclean, she will destroy, and a grievous destruction.

If only a man walking Wind and falsehood has falsified, 
I will drip to you, to the wine and to the strong-drink; 
and he will be the dripping of this people.

collecting I will collect the remnant of Struggles-with-God (Israel), 
together I will place them like the flock of Enclosure (Bozrah);

like a herd in the midst of his fold,

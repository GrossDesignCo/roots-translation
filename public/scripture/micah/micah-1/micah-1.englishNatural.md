
The word of He-Who-Is (YHWH) which was to Who-Is-Like-He (Micah) the Possessionite (Morashtite), in the days of He-Is-Whole (Jotham), Seized (Ahaz), He-Intensifies (Hezekiah), kings of Praise (Judah); 
which he has gazed over Keep (Samaria) and Foundation-of-Peace (Jerusalem).

"Hear, peoples, all of them;
hearken, earth and her fullness;

and let Lord He-Who-Is (YHWH) be in you to a witness, 
Lord from the temple of his holiness.

that behold, He-Who-Is (YHWH) is going-out from his place;
and he will go-down and tread over the high-places of earth.

and the mountains will be melted under him, 
and the valleys will break-open, 
like wax from the faces of the fire, 
like waters cascaded in a descent.

In the transgression of Heel-Grasper (Jacob) all this, 
and in the sins of the house of Struggles-with-God (Israel);
who is the transgression of Heel-Grasper (Jacob)? 
is it not Keep (Samaria)? 
and who are the high-places of Praise (Judah)? 
is it not Foundation-of-Peace (Jerusalem)?

And I will place Keep (Samaria) to a heap of the field, 
to plantings of vineyard;
and I will cascade to the valley her stones, 
and her foundations I will reveal.

And all her carved-images will be beaten, 
and all her hires will be incinerated in the fire, 
and all her idols I will place desolation;
that from the hire of a harlot, she has collected, 
and until the hire of a harlot they will return.

Over this I will lament and I will howl, 
I will walk spoiled and naked;
I will make a lamentation like jackals, 
and a mourning like daughters of ostrich.

that incurable, her blows;
that she has come until Praise (Judah);
he has touched until the gate of my people, until Foundation-of-Peace (Jerusalem).

In Winepress (Gath) you will declare not; 
weeping, you will weep not; 
in the house to Dust (Aphrah), dust, wallow yourself.

Pass-through to you, the sitting of Fair (Saphir), nakedness shame;
the sitting of Sheepfold (Zaanan) has brought-out not;

the lamentation of the house of the-Beside (Ezel) will take from you his standing.

that the sitting of Bitternesses (Maroth) has been sick to good;

that bad has gone-down from with He-Who-Is (YHWH) to the gate of Foundation-of-Peace (Jerusalem).

Harness the chariot to the steed, sitting one of Invincible (Lachish);

she is the beginning of sin to the daughter of Marker (Zion);

that in you have been found the transgressions of Struggles-with-God (Israel).

Therefore you will give send-offs, over Possession (Moresheth) Winepress (Gath); 
the houses of Lie (Achzib) to a lie, to the kings of Struggles-with-God (Israel).

Again I will bring the one inheriting to you, sitting one of Inheritance (Mareshah);
until Refuge (Adullam) the glory of Struggles-with-God (Israel) will come.

Make bald and shear, over the sons of your luxuries;
Widen your baldness like an eagle, 
that they have revealed from you."

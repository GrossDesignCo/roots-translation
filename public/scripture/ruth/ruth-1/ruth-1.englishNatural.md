
And it was in the days of the justice-doing of the Just-Ones, 
and famine was in the land; 
and a man walked from the House of Bread (Bethlehem) Praise (Judah), 
to sojourn in the fields of From-Father (Moab); 
he and his woman and the two of his sons.

And the name of the man, God-My-King (Elimelech), 
and the name of his woman, My-Pleasant (Naomi), 
and the name of the two of his sons, Sickness (Mahlon) and Wasting (Chilion); Fruitful-Ones (Ephrathites) from the House of Bread (Bethlehem) Praise (Judah), 
and they came the fields of From-Father (Moab) 
and were there.

And God-My-King (Elimelech) the man of My-Pleasant (Naomi) died, 
and she was left, and the two of her sons.

And they lifted to them women Moabite-Women, 
the name of the one Neck (Orpah) 
and the name of the second Friend (Ruth), 
and they sat there like ten years.

And also the two of them Sickness (Mahlon) and Wasting (Chilion) died; 
and the woman was left, from the two of her children and from her man.

And she and her daughters-in-law stood-up 
and returned from the fields of From-Father (Moab), 
that she had heard in the field of From-Father (Moab) 
that He-Who-Is (YHWH) had visited his people 
to give to them bread.

And she went-out from the place which she had been there, 
and the two of her daughters-in-law with her, 
and they walked in the way to return to the land of Praise (Judah).

And My-Pleasant (Naomi) said to the two of her daughters-in-law, 
"Go return, woman to the house of her mother; He-Who-Is (YHWH) make with you steadfast-love as which you made with the dead and with me.

He-Who-Is (YHWH) give to you, and find rest, woman the house of her man." 
And she kissed to them, and they lifted their voice and wept.
And they said to her, "That with you we will return to your people."

And My-Pleasant (Naomi) said, "Return, my daughters, why will you walk with me; are there again to me sons in my belly, and would they be to you men?"
Return, my daughters, go, that I have grown old from being to a man; that I have said, there-is to me hope, also I have been the night to a man, and also I have birthed sons,
Would you wait for them until which they would grow? Would you be bound for them without being to a man? No, my daughters; for very bitter to me from you, for the hand of He-Who-Is (YHWH)
has gone out in me." And they lifted their voice, and wept again: And Neck (Orpah) 
kissed to her mother-in-law; but Friend (Ruth) clung in her.
And she said, "Behold, your sister-in-law has returned to her people and to her gods; Return after your sister-in-law."
And Friend (Ruth) said, "You will encounter me not to leave you, to return from after you, for to which you will walk, I will walk; and in which you will lodge, I will lodge; your people, my people, and your God, my God.
where you will die, I will die, and there I will be buried; thus He-Who-Is (YHWH) will do to me, and thus he will add, that the death will part between me and between you."
And she saw that she was steadfast to go with her, and she ceased to speak to her.

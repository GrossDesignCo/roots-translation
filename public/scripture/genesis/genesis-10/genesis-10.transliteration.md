
veEleh toledot beney-Noach
Shem Cham vaYafet
vayivaledu lahem banim achar haMabbul

beney Yafet
Gomer uMagog uMadai veYavan veTuval uMeshech veTiras

uBeney Gomer
Ashkenaz veRiphath veTogarmah

uBeney Yavan
Elishah veTarshish
Kittim veDodanim

meEleh niphredu iyyei haGoyim beArtzotam
ish liLeshono leMishpechotam beGoyeihem

uBeney Cham
Khush uMitzrayim uPut uKenaan

uBeney Khush
Seba vaChavilah veSabtah veRaamah veSabteca
uBeney Raamah Sheba uDedan

veKhush yalad et-Nimrod
hu hechel lihyot gibbor baAretz

hu-hayah gibbor-tsayid liFney YHWH
al-ken yeamar
keNimrod gibbor tsayid liFney YHWH

vatehi reshit mamlakhto Babel
veErekh veAkkad veKhalneh
beEretz Shinar

min-haAretz hahi yatsa Ashur 
vayYiven et-Nineveh
veEt-Rechoboth ir veEt-Kalach veEt-Resen 
beyn Nineveh uveyn Kalach 
hi haIr haGedolah

uMitzrayim yalad et-Ludim veEt-Anamim veEt-Lehabim veEt-Naphtuhim

veEt-Pathrusim veEt-Casluhim asher yatsau miSham Pelishtim veEt-Kaphtorim

uKenaan yalad et-Tsidon bekhoro veEt-Chet

veEt-haYebusi veEt-haEmori veEt haGirgashi

veEt-haChivi veEt-haArqi veEt-haSini

veEt-haArvadi veEt-haTsemari veEt-haChamathi
veAchar nafotsu mishpechot haKenaani

vaYehi gevul haKenaani miTsidon boakhah Gerarah ad-Azzah
boakhah Sedomah vaAmorah veAdmah uTsevoyim ad-Lasha

eleh benei-Cham
leMishpechotam liLeshonotam beArtzotam beGoyeihem

uleShem yulad gam-hu avi kol-benei-Ever
achi Yafet haGadol

benei Shem
Elam veAshur veArpachshad veLud vaAram

uBenei Aram
Uts veChul veGether vaMash

veArpachshad yalad et-Shelach
veShelach yalad et-Ever

uleEver yulad shenei banim
shem haEchad Peleg ki beYamav niphlagah haAretz
veShem achiv Yoqtan

veYoqtan yalad et-Almodad veEt-Shalef veEt-Chatsarmavet veEt-Yarach

veEt-Hadoram veEt-Uzal veEt-Diqlah

veEt-Oval veEt-Avimael veEt-Sheba

veEt-Ophir veEt-Chavilah veEt-Yovav
kol-eleh benei Yoqtan

vaYehi moshavam miMesha boakhah Sefarah har haQedem

eleh benei-Shem
leMishpechotam liLeshonotam beArtzotam leGoyeihem

eleh mishpechot benei-Noach
leToledotam beGoyeihem


And these are the birthings of the sons of Rest (Noah):
Name (Shem), Hot (Ham), and Spacious (Japheth);
and to them were birthed sons, after the flood.

The sons of Spacious (Japheth):
Complete (Gomer), and From-Gog (Magog), and Measure (Madai), and Mire (Javan), and World (Tubal), and Drawing (Meshech), and Moist (Tiras).

And the sons of Complete (Gomer):
Fire-scatter (Ashkenaz), and Sink (Riphath), and Bone-all (Togarmah).

And the sons of Mire (Javan):
God-helps (Elishah), and golden-stone (Tarshish);
Bruisers (Kittim), and Beloveds (Dodanim).

From these have been parted the islands of the nations in their lands;
a man to his tongue, to their families, in their nations.

And the sons of Hot (Ham):
Black (Kush), and Double-Narrows (Egypt), and Bow (Put), and Low (Canaan).

And the sons of Black (Kush):
Drunkard (Seba), and Strength (Havilah), and Striking (Sabtah), and Thunder (Raamah), and Striking-you (Sabteca);
and the sons of Thunder (Raamah): Oath (Sheba), and Lowland (Dedan).

And Black (Kush) birthed Rebel (Nimrod);
he caused to begin to be a mighty-one in the land.

He was a mighty-one of the hunter to the face of He-Who-Is (YHWH);
so moreover it will be said,
"Like Rebel (Nimrod), a mighty-one, a hunter to the face of He-Who-Is (YHWH)."

and the heading of his kingdom was Confusion (Babylon),
and Length (Erech), and Fortress (Akkadia), and Fortress (Calneh),
in the land of Tooth-of-Two (Shinar).

from that land brought-out Steppe (Assyria), 
and he built Dwelling-of-Fish (Nineveh)
and Open-Places (Rehoboth) City and Vigor (Calah), and Bridle (Resen), 
between Dwelling-of-Fish (Nineveh) and between Vigor (Calah); 
this, The City, The Great. 
And Double-Narrows (Egypt) birthed Luds (Ludim), and Ans (Anamim), and Flames (Lehabim), and Of-Ptah (Naphtuhim),

and Southerners (Pathrusim), and Loiners (Casluhim), which they have brought-out from there Wallowers (Philistines), and Palm-knobs (Caphtorim).

And Low (Canaan) birthed Fishery (Sidon), his firstborn, and Heth (Heth),

and the Jebusite, and the Amorite, and the Girgashite,

and the Hivite, and the Arkite, and the Sinite,

and the Arvadite, and the Zemarite, and the Hamathite;
and after have been spread-out the families of the Canaanite.
And the border of the Canaanite 
was from Fishery (Sidon), as you come toward Gerar (Gerar) until Strong (Gaza);
as you come toward Devastation (Sodom), and toward Gomorrah (Gomorrah), and Earth (Admah), and Gazelles (Zeboim) until Lasha (Lasha).

These are the sons of Hot (Ham),
to their families, to their tongues, in their lands, in their nations.

And to Name (Shem) was birthed also he, father of all the sons of Crossing (Eber),
brother of Spacious (Japheth) the great.

The sons of Name (Shem):
Hidden (Elam), and Steppe (Assyria), and Heal-Shaddai (Arpachshad), and Lud (Lud), and High (Aram).

And the sons of High (Aram):
Counsel (Uz), and Writhe (Hul), and Gether (Gether), and Feel (Mash).

And Heal-Shaddai (Arpachshad) birthed Sent (Shelah);
and Sent (Shelah) birthed Crossing (Eber).

And to Crossing (Eber) was birthed two sons;
the name of the one Channel (Peleg), that in his days the land
has been divided; and the name of his brother Small (Joktan).

And Small (Joktan) birthed Almodad (Almodad), and Drawn (Sheleph), and Court-of-Death (Hazarmaveth), and Moon-cycle (Jerah),

and Honor (Hadoram), and Uzal (Uzal), and Palm (Diklah),

and Obal (Obal), and Father-is-God (Abimael), and Oath (Sheba),

and Ophir (Ophir), and Strength (Havilah), and Howler (Jobab);
all these are the sons of Small (Joktan).
And their seat from Freedom (Mesha) 
was, as you come toward Counting (Sephar), the mountain of the east.

These are the sons of Name (Shem),
to their families, to their tongues, in their lands, to their nations.

These are the families of the sons of Rest (Noah),
to their birthings, in their nations;
and from these have been parted the nations in the land after the flood.


vayVarekh Elohim et-Noach veEt-banav
vayYomer lahem 
peru uRevu uMileu et-haAretz

uMorakhem veChitkhem yihyeh al kol-chayat haAretz
veAl kol-of haShamayim
beKhol asher tirmos haAdamah
uVekhol-degei haYam
beYedkhem nitanu

kol-remes asher hu-chai lakhem yihyeh leOkhlah
keYereq esev natati lakhem et-kol

akh-basar beNafsho damo lo tokhelu

veAkh et-dimkhem leNafshotekhem edrosh
miYad kol-chayah edreshennu
uMiYad haAdam
miYad ish achiv
edrosh et-nefesh haAdam

shofekh dam haAdam
baAdam damo yishafekh
ki beTselem Elohim asah et-haAdam

veAtem peru urevu
shirtzu baAretz urevu-vah

vayYomer Elohim el-Noach 
veEl-banav ito lemor

vaAni hineni meqim et-beriti itkhem 
veEt-zarkhem acharekhem
veEt kol-nefesh haChayah asher itkhem
baOph baBehemah uvKhol-chayat haAretz itkhem
miKol yotzei haTevah leKhol chayat haAretz
vaHaqimoti 
et-beriti itkhem
veLo-yikaret kol-basar od mimei haMabbul
veLo-yihyeh od mabbul leshachet haAretz

vayYomer Elohim zot ot-haBerit asher-ani noten
beyni uvEynekhem
uveyn kol-nefesh chayah asher itkhem
ledorot olam

et-qashti natati beAnan
veHayetah leOt berit
beyni uveyn haAretz

veHayah beAnnani anan al-haAretz
veNiretah haQeshet beAnan

veZakharti et-beriti asher beyni uvEynekhem uveyn kol-nefesh chayah beKol-basar
veLo-yihyeh od haMayim leMabbul leshachet kol-basar

veHayetah haQeshet beAnan
uReitihah leZkor berit olam beyn Elohim uveyn kol-nefesh chayah beKol-basar asher al-haAretz

vayYomer Elohim el-Noach zot ot-haBerit asher haQimoti beyni uveyn kol-basar asher al-haAretz

vaYihyu beney-Noach haYotzeim min-haTevah Shem veCham vaYafet

veCham hu avi Kenaan

shloshah eleh beney-Noach
uMeEleh nafatsah kol-haAretz

vaYachel Noach ish haAdamah 
vaYitta kerem

vaYisht min-haYayin vaYishkar

vaYitgal beTokh ahaloh

vaYar, Cham avi Kenaan, et, ervat aviv; 
vaYagged liShney-echav, baChutz.

vaYiqach Shem vaYephet et-haSimlah, 
vaYasimu al-shekhem shneihem, vaYelkhu achoranit, vaYekhasu et ervat avihem; 
uPneihem, achoranit, veErvat avihem, lo rau.

vaYiQets Noach miYeyno

vaYeda et asher-asah lo beno haQatan

vayYomer arur Kenaan eved avadim yihyeh leAchav

vayYomer barukh YHWH elohei Shem
vihi Kenaan eved lamo 
yapht Elohim leYafet veYishkon beAhalei-shem vihi Kenaan eved lamo

vayechi-Noach achar haMabbul
shelosh meot shanah vachamishim shanah

vayihyu kol-yemey-Noach tesha meot shanah vachamishim shanah
vayamot


vayHi khol-haAretz safah echat uDevarim achadim

vayHi beNosam miQedem 
vayYimtse'u biqah beEretz ShinAr 
vayYeshvu sham

vayYomeru ish el-reeyhu 
havah nilvnah levenim veNisrefah liSrefah 
vatTehi lahem haLevenah leEven 
veHacheymar hayah lahem laChomer

vayYomeru havah nivneh-lanu ir uMigdal 
veRosho baShamayim 
veNaaseh-lanu shem 
pen-naFuts al-peney kol-haAretz

vayYered YHWH liRot et-haIr 
veEt-haMigdal asher banu beney haAdam

vaYomer YHWH 
hen am echad 
veSafah achat leKhulam
veZeh hachilam laAsot 
veAttah lo-yibatser mehem kol asher yazmu laAsot

havah nerdah 
veNavlah sham sefatam
asher lo yishme'u ish sefat reehu

vayYafetz YHWH otam misham al-peney khol-haAretz
vayachdelu livnot haIr

al-ken qara shemah Babel
ki-sham balal YHWH sefat kol-haAretz
umisham hephitzam YHWH al-peney kol-haAretz

eleh toledot Shem

Shem ben-meat shanah vaYoled et-Arpachshad shanatayim achar haMabbul
vaYechi-Shem acharey holido et-Arpachshad
chamesh meot shanah
vaYoled banim uVanot

veArpachshad chai chamesh uShloshim shanah
vaYoled et-Shelach
vaYechi Arpachshad acharey holido et-Shelach
shalosh shanim veArba meot shanah
vaYoled banim uVanot

veShelach chai shloshim shanah
vaYoled et-Eber
vaYechi-Shelach acharey holido et-Ever
shalosh shanim veArba meot shanah
vaYoled banim uVanot

vaYechi-Eber arba uShloshim shanah
vaYoled et-Peleg
vaYechi-Eber acharey holido et-Peleg
shloshim shanah veArba meot shanah
vaYoled banim uVanot

vaYechi-Peleg shloshim shanah
vaYoled et-Reu
vaYechi-Peleg acharey holido et-Reu
tesha shanim uMatayim shanah
vaYoled banim uVanot

vaYechi Reu shtayim uShloshim shanah
vaYoled et-Serug
vaYechi Reu acharey holido et-Serug
sheva shanim uMatayim shanah
vaYoled banim uVanot

vaYechi Serug shloshim shanah
vaYoled et-Nachor
vaYechi Serug acharey holido et-Nachor
matayim shanah
vaYoled banim uVanot

vaYechi Nachor tesha veEsrim shanah
vaYoled et-Terach
vaYechi Nachor acharey holido et-Terach
tesha-esreh shanah uMeat shanah
vaYoled banim uVanot

vaYechi-Terach shivim shanah
vaYoled et-Abram et-Nachor veEt-Haran

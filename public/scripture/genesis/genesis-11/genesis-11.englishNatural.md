And all the land was, one lip and of one words.

And it was in their journeying from the east, 
and they found a plain in the land of Tooth-of-Two (Shinar) 
and they settled there.

And they said, man to his friend, 
"Come let us make-bricks bricks and let us incinerate to an incinerating." 
And the brick was to them for stone, 
and the bitumen was to them for mortar.

And they said, "Come let us build for us a city and a tower 
and its head in the heavens, 
and let us make for us a name 
lest we are scattered over the face of all the land."

And He-Who-Is (YHWH) descended to see the city 
and the tower which the sons of the Earthling (Adam) had built.

And He-Who-Is (YHWH) said, 
"Behold, the people is one 
and one lip to all of them,
and this, the beginning of their making; 
and now nothing will be restrained from them, 
all which they will plan to make.

Come let us descend, 
and let us confuse there the lip of them,
which man will hear not the lip of his friend."

And He-Who-Is (YHWH) scattered them from there 
over the face of all the land;
and they ceased to build the city. So moreover he called the name of it Confusion (Babylon),
that there He-Who-Is (YHWH) confused the lip of all the land;
and from there He-Who-Is (YHWH) scattered them over the face of all the land. 
These are the birthings of Name (Shem);

Name (Shem), a son of a hundred years, and birthed Heal-Shaddai (Arpachshad) two years after the flood.
And Name (Shem) lived after he begot Heal-Shaddai (Arpachshad),
five hundred years,
and birthed sons and daughters.

And Heal-Shaddai (Arpachshad) lived five and thirty years,
and birthed Sent (Shelah).
And Heal-Shaddai (Arpachshad) lived after he birthed Sent (Shelah),
three years and four hundred years,
and birthed sons and daughters.

And Sent (Shelah) lived thirty years,
and birthed Crossing (Eber).
And Sent (Shelah) lived after he begot Crossing (Eber),
three years and four hundred years,
and begot sons and daughters.

And Crossing (Eber) lived four and thirty years,
and birthed Channel (Peleg).
And Crossing (Eber) lived after he begot Channel (Peleg),
thirty years and four hundred years,
and begot sons and daughters.

And Channel (Peleg) lived thirty years,
and birthed Friend (Reu).
And Channel (Peleg) lived after he begot Friend (Reu),
nine years and two hundred years,
and birthed sons and daughters.

And Friend (Reu) lived two and thirty years,
and birthed Intertwined (Serug).
And Friend (Reu) lived after he begot Intertwined (Serug),
seven years and two hundred years,
and begot sons and daughters.

And Intertwined (Serug) lived thirty years,
and birthed Snorting (Nahor).
And Intertwined (Serug) lived after he begot Snorting (Nahor),
two hundred years,
and begot sons and daughters.

And Snorting (Nahor) lived nine and twenty years,
and birthed Delay (Terah).
And Snorting (Nahor) lived after he begot Delay (Terah),
nine ten years and a hundred years,
and begot sons and daughters.

And Delay (Terah) lived seventy years,
and birthed Exalted-Father (Abram), Snorting (Nahor), and Mountaineer (Haran).
